EYE SIGN RECORDER - DISCLAIMER
============================================================

PLEASE READ THIS BEFORE YOU START

This program is NOT a medical test.
It cannot diagnose myasthenia gravis or any other illness.

What it actually does:
  - It guides you through a set of simple eye movements.
  - It uses your camera to measure things like how far your
    eyelid droops, and how steady your eyes are.
  - It saves those measurements so you can show them to your
    doctor, and so you can compare one day to another.

What it CANNOT do:
  - It cannot tell you whether you are ill.
  - It cannot tell you whether you are getting better or worse.
  - It cannot replace an examination by a doctor.
  - It cannot be used to decide whether to change medication.

The measurements can be wrong. Poor lighting, glasses, a moving
camera, tiredness, or simply sitting closer to the screen can all
change the numbers without anything changing about you.

GET MEDICAL HELP IMMEDIATELY - do not use this program - if you have:
  - Trouble breathing, or shortness of breath when lying flat
  - Trouble swallowing, or choking on food or drink
  - A weak or breathless voice that is getting worse
  - Sudden severe weakness in your neck, arms or legs
  - Sudden double vision with a bad headache, or a droopy eyelid
    with a large pupil on the same side

These can be emergencies. This program will not detect them.

Your video is not uploaded anywhere. It is processed on this
computer only. By default no video is saved - only numbers.

Privacy:
  - Video frames are analysed in memory and then discarded.
  - Only numeric measurements are written to disk, in the folder you
    choose. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  - If you turn on 'save video clips', those clips stay on this
    computer. They are identifiable. Protect them like medical records.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT APPEARS ON EVERY REPORT
------------------------------------------------------------

This document was produced by a consumer webcam program. It is a
RECORD OF AN OBSERVATION SESSION, not a clinical investigation.

The measurements have not been validated against any clinical
standard. They are not comparable to results from a hospital
eye-movement laboratory, Hess chart, tensilon test, ice-pack test,
repetitive nerve stimulation, single-fibre EMG, or antibody testing.

No value in this document should be used on its own to support or
exclude any diagnosis, or to guide treatment.

------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN LIMITATION OF EACH ITEM
------------------------------------------------------------

fatigable_ptosis:
  Eyelid position also falls with ordinary tiredness, dry eyes, age-related lid changes and sleepiness. A downward trend here is not specific to anything.

cogan_lid_twitch:
  A webcam running at 30 frames per second can easily miss a real lid twitch, and can easily invent one out of a blink. Absence of a twitch here means nothing at all.

curtain_sign:
  This item needs another person to hold an eyelid up. Any pressure on the eye or the brow changes the result. Never press on an eye.

peek_sign:
  Gentle closure means gentle. If you squeeze your eyes shut the test is invalid. Voluntary relaxation also reopens the eyes.

ophthalmoparesis:
  This estimates eye position from a flat camera image. It cannot measure a true ocular ductions deficit and cannot identify which muscle or nerve is involved.

fatigable_saccades:
  Peak saccade velocity cannot be measured properly below about 120 frames per second. On a normal webcam only large amplitude changes are meaningful, and even those are rough.

gaze_holding:
  Small camera shake, head movement and image noise all look like eye drift to this program.

head_posture:
  A head tilt has many causes, including habit, neck problems, hearing, and the position of your screen.

variability:
  Differences between sessions are dominated by how you sat, the lighting, and the camera - usually far more than by your eyes.
