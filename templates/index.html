CAMERA-ASSISTED PROTOTYPE

Eye Movement Observation

A guided camera session that records observable eye and head-movement changes and summarizes the session at the end.

Ready
Important: This is a research/prototype tool, not a diagnostic test. It measures observable eye-position and eye-movement patterns from the camera. It does not diagnose MG. The ptosis-related output is a webcam-based observational estimate, not a clinical diagnosis.

Consent for this prototype session

Your camera is used in this browser to perform the eye-movement observation. This prototype does not send the live camera video to the server. If you paste MG questionnaire results below, those results are processed in this browser to create the combined summary.
